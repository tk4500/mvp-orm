### Priority Issues
- **[P2] Missing Enter-Key Support on Barcode Input**
  - **Why it matters**: Field operators using physical Bluetooth/USB barcode scanners rely on the scanner simulating an "Enter" keystroke at the end of the scan string. Without a keyboard listener, hardware scans will idle in the input box.
  - **Fix**: Add an inline keydown listener (`onkeypress="if(event.key === 'Enter') handleScanAction()"`) to the `#manual-qr-input`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden` or `/impeccable polish`

- **[P3] Toast Z-Index Collision Risk**
  - **Why it matters**: Both `#toast-container` and `#app-confirm-modal` use `z-[60]`. If an error toast fires simultaneously with a modal, the modal backdrop might occlude the toast.
  - **Fix**: Elevate the toast container to `z-[70]`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

### Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (The Impatient Operator)**: Alex uses a physical scanner gun attached to a tablet. If the gun doesn't automatically trigger form submission on "Enter" (P2), Alex will have to touch the screen manually after every single scan, causing immense friction.
- **Casey (The Precise Admin)**: In the "Ajustar Estoque" modal, Casey sets an *absolute* value. They might prefer entering a *delta* (e.g., "+15" or "-2") because recounting the entire warehouse shelf just to report a few damaged items is frustrating.
