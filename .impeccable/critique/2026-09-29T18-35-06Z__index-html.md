## Design Specificity Verdict
- **LLM Assessment**: The interface successfully establishes a professional, high-end logistics control center aesthetic. The dual-depot view and Android field scanner simulation provide immediate domain authenticity.
- **Deterministic Scan**: Detected 13 `cramped-padding` warnings inside `.double-bezel-inner` containers, 1 `flat-type-hierarchy` warning, and 1 `pulsing-dot` error on the live sync indicator.

## Overall Impression
NexusLog is a robust, well-structured operational dashboard. With refinement of container padding and removal of decorative pulsing elements, it will achieve Awwwards-tier polish.

## What's Working
1. Split-screen inventory comparison between Depósito 01 and Depósito 05.
2. Simulated Android scanner with interactive depot switching and quick-scan buttons.
3. Secure admin override protocol with audit trail logging.

## Priority Issues
- **[P1] What**: Cramped padding inside container components (`.double-bezel-inner`).
  - **Why it matters**: Content sits too close to borders, reducing visual breathing room.
  - **Fix**: Increase inner padding to `p-6` or `p-8` universally across card components.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`
- **[P2] What**: Decorative pulsing status dot on sync indicator.
  - **Why it matters**: Violates anti-slop guidelines regarding static liveness simulations.
  - **Fix**: Replace with a solid status badge or timestamp.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

## Persona Red Flags
- **Jordan (First-Timer)**: Missing inline tooltips explaining the transition from Depot 01 to Depot 05 disassembly.
- **Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: Warehouse scanner action buttons should be larger and optimized for one-handed thumb reach on mobile viewports.

## Minor Observations
- Ensure all modal dialogs support keyboard ESC dismissal.
- Add tabular number alignment (`tabular-nums`) to all financial and inventory quantities.
